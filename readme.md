## Overview

EXO is a governance token originally developed within the Exohood blockchain ecosystem.

The token was deployed on Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, and Polygon.

Its designated purpose is participation in governance proposals associated with the Exoswap protocol, where the applicable voting mechanisms are available.

EXO does not represent ownership of Exohood Labs Inc., company shares, financial reserves, or rights to company profits.

The token is not backed by cash, real estate, securities, or other assets.

**EXO is not an investment product offered by Exohood Labs.**
